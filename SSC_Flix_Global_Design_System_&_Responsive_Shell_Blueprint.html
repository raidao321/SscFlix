Mobile Ergonomic Rules & Touch Target Matrix

44px Minimal Tap Boundaries: All icon buttons, segmented chips, and navigation destinations have active hit areas ≥ 44px × 44px to eliminate mis-taps on mobile devices.
Fixed Bottom Occlusion Buffer: The scroll area enforces a mandatory pb-28 (112px) spacer so floating buttons or last question options are never covered by the bottom bar.
Typography Minimums: Headings ~22px, Question Text ≥ 15px, Body 14px, Labels 14px, Secondary/Metadata 12px, Bottom Nav Labels 12px.
